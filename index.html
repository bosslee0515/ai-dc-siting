要素定义（纳入）边界（排除或单列）
P 研究对象
  • 物理数据中心设施：超大规模、云、托管、企业、边缘（需有地理布局）。
  • AI 数据中心、AI 训练/推理集群、AI 服务器部署。
  • 算力基础设施与"东数西算"枢纽。
  • 作为"大型负荷"出现在电力系统中的数据中心。
  • 类比对象：加密挖矿等大型灵活负荷（单列）。
  • 机房内部设备摆放、虚拟机/容器/服务放置、副本与缓存放置、SDN 控制器放置：排除。
  • 基站侧边缘服务器部署：仅当以地理设施形式选址时列为边界文献。
C 核心概念
  1. 选址决策：选址、区位选择、设施选址/选址-分配、布局优化、适宜性评价、选址与定容、接入容量与节点选择、共址/表后发电、规划者主动选址。
  2. 选址相关的空间证据：电网接入与可接入容量、水压力与水足迹、碳/边际排放、空气污染与健康、热岛、气候与冷却、灾害、土地与分区、社区与公平、地方经济、时延与光纤。
  3. 方法：GIS-MCDA、数学优化（MILP、多目标、鲁棒/随机）、容量扩展/电力系统模型、计量与因果识别、机器学习/GeoAI、遥感。
  • 仅研究运行调度、负荷转移、温控、能效而无地理选址决策：排除（E2）。
  • 仅给出全国总量而无空间维度：排除（E4）。
  • 足迹核算类文献：列入 A 约束（背景证据）。
C 情境
  • 全球任意国家或区域；空间尺度从建筑/地块、普查区/县、电网节点、省/州到国家。
  • 时间：A 流不设下限；B 方法流限 2021 年以后。
  • 文献类型：期刊、会议、书章、预印本与工作论文（灰色文献单独标注）。
  • 期刊分区不作为纳入条件，只用于分层展示。
  • B 方法流出于工作量考虑只取一区或知名期刊。
证据流
  • A 直接：数据中心的位置、数量或容量是决策变量或评价对象。
  • A 约束/背景：为选址提供空间约束、影响或驱动因素证据。
  • B 方法迁移：其他设施中可迁移的 GeoAI、MCDA 稳健性、鲁棒/公平/多目标选址方法。
三条流分别计数。
ElementDefinition (include)Boundary (exclude or list separately)
P Population
  • Physical data-centre facilities: hyperscale, cloud, colocation, enterprise, edge (with a geographic layout).
  • AI data centres, AI training/inference clusters, AI server deployment.
  • Computing-power infrastructure and the East-Data-West-Computing hubs.
  • Data centres as "large loads" in power systems.
  • Analogues: crypto-mining and other large flexible loads (listed separately).
  • In-room equipment layout, VM/container/service placement, replica/cache placement, SDN controller placement: excluded.
  • Edge servers at base stations: boundary items only when sited as geographic facilities.
C Concept
  1. Siting decisions: siting, location choice, facility location / location-allocation, layout optimisation, suitability evaluation, siting-and-sizing, interconnection capacity and node choice, co-location / behind-the-meter supply, planner-initiated siting.
  2. Spatial evidence relevant to siting: grid access and hosting capacity, water stress and footprint, carbon / marginal emissions, air pollution and health, heat islands, climate and cooling, hazards, land and zoning, community and equity, local economy, latency and fibre.
  3. Methods: GIS-MCDA, mathematical optimisation (MILP, multi-objective, robust/stochastic), capacity-expansion / power-system models, econometrics and causal inference, ML/GeoAI, remote sensing.
  • Operations only (scheduling, load shifting, thermal control, efficiency) with no siting decision: excluded (E2).
  • National totals with no spatial dimension: excluded (E4).
  • Footprint accounting: kept as A-constraint (background evidence).
C Context
  • Any country or region; scales from building/parcel, census tract/county, grid node, province/state to nation.
  • Time: no lower limit for stream A; stream B limited to 2021 onwards.
  • Types: journal, conference, book chapter, preprints and working papers (grey literature flagged).
  • Journal tier is not an inclusion criterion; it is recorded and used for display only.
  • Stream B restricted to top-tier or well-known journals to keep the workload manageable.
Evidence streams
  • A-direct: the location, number or capacity of data centres is a decision variable or evaluation object.
  • A-constraint/background: evidence on spatial constraints, impacts or drivers relevant to siting.
  • B-method transfer: transferable GeoAI, MCDA-robustness, robust/equitable/multi-objective siting methods from other facilities.
The three streams are counted separately.

Why Scopus was not searched directly: Scopus and WoS require an institutional login and API key, which I cannot access.

Known OpenAlex limitations:

筛选规则Screening rules

  1. 文献类型：排除学位论文、数据集、勘误、撤稿、社论、书评；预印本与报告标为灰色文献。
  2. 规则初筛（精确正则）：A 直接候选＝数据中心/AI 算力词 × 选址/接入/布局词，排除运行类主导记录；A 约束候选＝题名含数据中心 × 空间词 × 约束词；B 方法＝B 组检索或旧库 B 类，一区或知名期刊、2021 年以后。
  3. 题名人工筛选：2,755 条。
  4. 摘要逐篇复核：496 条，逐条给出纳排、原因、证据流、主题、是否 AI 专门、区域、研究问题与贡献。
  5. 语义编码（v2）：453 篇纳入文献逐篇按题名+摘要标注维度与方法代码，并写英文研究问题与贡献。
  6. 期刊层级（v2）：旧库已核的中科院/JCR 分区优先；其余期刊用 OpenAlex 代理分位；分区只用于展示，不作纳排依据。
  1. Document type: theses, datasets, errata, retractions, editorials and book reviews excluded; preprints and reports flagged as grey literature.
  2. Rule-based pre-screen (exact regex): A-direct candidates = data-centre/AI-compute terms × siting/interconnection/layout terms, excluding operations-dominated records; A-constraint candidates = data centre in title × spatial term × constraint term; B-method = B queries or old-library B class, top-tier or well-known journals, 2021+.
  3. Manual title screening: 2,755 records.
  4. Abstract review: 496 records, each with decision, reason, stream, theme, AI-specific flag, region, research question and contribution.
  5. Semantic coding (v2): all 453 included records coded for dimensions and methods from title + abstract, with English research questions and contributions.
  6. Journal tier (v2): CAS/JCR tiers verified in the old library take priority; other journals get an OpenAlex proxy quartile; tiers are for display, not for inclusion.

Q1 "哪里适合建？"——多准则 / GIS 适宜性评价Q1 "Where is suitable?" — MCDA / GIS suitability

A 直接 T1： 篇。

  • 典型问题：在候选地（城市、省、栅格）中按专家或数据给出的权重排出适宜性次序。
  • 演进脉络：纯专家打分（AHP 、层次决策模型 、ELECTRE TRI ）→ 模糊 MCDM（）→ GIS 栅格叠加 + 数据驱动权重（随机森林定权 、英国全国适宜性面 、欧洲 NUTS-2 含权重不确定性 、多源遥感 + 帕累托承载力 ）。
  • AI 时代的新问题：电网就绪度作为准则 ；"可行性优先"、把电网可送达与水余量设为硬约束 ；"还剩哪里能建" 。

A-direct T1: records.

  • Typical question: rank candidate places (cities, provinces, raster cells) using expert- or data-derived weights.
  • Trajectory: pure expert scoring (AHP , hierarchical decision model , ELECTRE TRI ) → fuzzy MCDM () → GIS raster overlay with data-driven weights (random-forest weights , UK national suitability surface , European NUTS-2 with weight uncertainty , multi-source remote sensing + Pareto carrying capacity ).
  • New AI-era questions: grid readiness as a criterion ; "feasibility first", with grid deliverability and water headroom as hard constraints ; "where can we still build?" .

Responses to the two review comments

Comment 1: "Why were the records from the new library not screened by journal tier?"
In v1 only journals from the old library (already verified in the ChatGPT round) were labelled top-tier; all journals in the new library were marked "unverified". The reason is that I cannot access the CAS journal ranking or the JCR database, and the SCImago (SJR) download was blocked by the site's anti-bot protection. What v2 does:
  • Where a new journal's ISSN matches a verified journal in the old library, the CAS 2025 + JCR 2025 tier is reused (tiers A and B).
  • For all other journals I computed an OpenAlex field-normalised proxy quartile: within the journal's main OpenAlex field (journals with > 500 works), journals are ranked by two-year mean citedness; the top 25% are proxy Q1, and so on (tiers C1–C3). Among included records: verified top tier , partially verified , proxy Q1 , Q2 , Q3–Q4 .
  • A proxy quartile is not a CAS or JCR tier; it is a rough stratification only. Verify in JCR / the CAS table with your university account before citing tiers in a paper.
  • Tier is still not an inclusion criterion: much of the newest AI data-centre siting work is 2026 preprints and working papers, and screening by tier would discard the research frontier.
Comment 2: "Why not tag and classify by the central meaning of title and abstract?"
The comment is right. The v1 coverage matrix used regex tags: any abstract mentioning "water" counted as water-related, even when water appeared only in the background, so counts were inflated. What v2 does:
  • I re-read the title and abstract of all 453 included records and assigned 14 dimension codes and 10 method codes by central meaning (see the coding table on the PCC page). A dimension is coded only when it is the object, a constraint or a finding of the study.
  • All numbers in the coverage matrix and gap table are now computed live from these codes; click any number to see the records.
  • Semantic coding gives more conservative results: e.g. A-direct records that address grid + water + carbon together fall from ~10 (v1 regex) to , and those that are also multi-objective from ~2 to .

Other limitations

  • Screener: one screener and coder (Claude), with no independent double screening. A formal review needs a second screener to check at least ~20% and report agreement (e.g. Cohen's κ).
  • Database coverage: Scopus, IEEE Xplore and ACM DL were not searched directly. Broad queries were truncated by relevance, so a few records may be missing.
